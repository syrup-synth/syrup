**Syrup open beta**: a wavetable synth and groovebox for the 3DS and 2DS. Also playable in any browser: https://syrup-synth.github.io/syrup/

This is a beta. Please report anything that breaks: open an issue and say which build you're on (shown on the splash screen and the HARDWARE page).

**Install:** FBI > Remote Install > Scan QR Code with `install-qr.png`, or install `syrup.cia` with FBI, or copy `syrup.3dsx` to `/3ds/`. Needs custom firmware (Luma3DS).

**Sound setup (once):** on first start Syrup offers to copy the sound chip's firmware from the console's own sound app (press A). Or press L + Down + Select (Rosalina menu) > Miscellaneous options > Dump DSP firmware, then reopen Syrup.

**What's in it:** two wavetable oscillators plus sub and noise, two filters, LFOs (classic or hand-drawn), macros and a mod matrix, 236 patches, a master effects chain, a piano-roll sequencer with 64-step patterns and song mode, chord pads, a 10-row drum sequencer, a four-track tape, Wi-Fi and Network MIDI with clock in and out, and a phone link (remote control or a full second Syrup on the 3DS's clock). The full list is in the README and `docs/REFERENCE.md`.
