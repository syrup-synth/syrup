Both screens take taps: on the top one, tap a setting to pick it and drag sideways to change it. Circle pad on the left (D-PAD switches it to the four arrows).
Keyboard: arrows = D-pad, I J K L = circle pad, X = A, Z = B, S = X, A = Y, Q / W = L / R, Shift = SELECT, Enter = START.
MIDI plays it from a controller (Chrome / Edge on a computer, or the Web MIDI Browser app on iPhone). Your sounds and drum grid are kept in this browser.
