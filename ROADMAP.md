# Roadmap

Planned after the open beta (not promises, and not in order):

- Standalone Mac / PC app, built from the web version
- Plugin version: CLAP, plus VST3 / AU
- Reverse tape (play tape tracks backwards)
- One-colour (monochrome) themes
- A demo session to open and pull apart
- A tracker-style VU mixer view
- Performance mode with punch-ins
- Stems export
- FM operator: a 4-operator FM oscillator (back once it runs well on the 2DS)

Ideas and bug reports are welcome as [issues](../../issues).
